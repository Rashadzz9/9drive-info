# 9Drive Privacy Policy

9Drive is a personal, self-hosted project used by its developer to manage his own Google Drive storage.

**Data accessed:** Google account email, profile name, and Google Drive files, only to upload, list and organize files in the user's own Drive.

**Data storage:** OAuth tokens are stored encrypted in a database on the developer's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

**Data deletion:** Access can be revoked anytime at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Contact: zbiyevrashad@gmail.com
